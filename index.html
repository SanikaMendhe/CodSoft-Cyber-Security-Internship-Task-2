UNDERSTAND THE THREAT

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack in which attackers pretend to be trusted people or organizations to trick users into revealing sensitive information.

📧

Email Phishing

Fake emails that pretend to come from banks, companies, or other trusted organizations.

📱

Smishing

Fraudulent SMS messages that may contain suspicious links or requests for personal information.

🎣

Spear Phishing

Targeted phishing messages designed for a specific person or organization.

🌐

Fake Websites

Websites designed to look like genuine login pages to steal passwords or other sensitive data.

KNOW THE PROCESS

How Does Phishing Work?

Understanding the attack process helps you recognize warning signs before becoming a victim.

01

Fake Message

An attacker sends a message pretending to be a trusted person or organization.

02

Create Urgency

The message may threaten account suspension or promise an unexpected reward.

03

Steal Information

The victim may be tricked into visiting a fake website or sharing sensitive information.

RED FLAGS

How to Identify a Phishing Message

⚠️

Urgent Language

Messages demanding immediate action or threatening account closure.

🔗

Suspicious Links

Unexpected links or web addresses that imitate legitimate websites.

🔑

Password Requests

Unexpected requests for passwords, OTPs, or banking information.

🎁

Unexpected Rewards

Messages claiming you won a prize you never entered to win.

PROTECT YOURSELF

Essential Cyber Safety Tips

Follow these practices to improve your online security.

🔍

Verify the Sender

Check the complete sender address and confirm unexpected requests through a trusted channel.

🚫

Avoid Unknown Links

Do not click unexpected links. Visit the official website by typing its address yourself.

🔐

Use Strong Passwords

Use unique passwords for different accounts and enable multi-factor authentication.

📢

Report Suspicious Messages

Report phishing attempts to the relevant service provider or your organization's security team.

🛡️

Remember: Never Share Your OTP

Do not share your OTP, password, or banking PIN with anyone who contacts you unexpectedly.
